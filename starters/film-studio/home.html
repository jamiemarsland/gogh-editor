— About us

A brand film should make someone feel something before it explains anything.

/01

Halcyon was started in 2014 by two documentary editors who were tired of watching brands play it safe. The first films were shot for community projects out of a borrowed room above a print shop.

Today we make campaign films, brand documentaries and content series for clients across the UK and Europe, and the belief that every brand has one honest story has not moved an inch.

— Process

Great production isn’t accidental. It’s designed.

A clear process is the first creative decision we make. It keeps projects on time, keeps clients informed and keeps the crew on the story rather than the chaos.

1

Discover

We start with your audience, your message and the feeling you want to leave behind. Every good film begins with a good brief.

2

Script and storyboard

We shape the story, scripted or documentary, and map every shot before a camera rolls. No surprises on set.

3

Production

One planned day, or a multi-country expedition: the crew captures the story with precision and presence.
