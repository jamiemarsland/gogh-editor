— About us

Two editors, a borrowed room, and a refusal to play it safe.

/01

Halcyon was started in 2014 by two documentary editors who had watched too many brands hide behind stock footage and a voiceover. The first films were made for community projects out of a borrowed room above a print shop.

Today the studio makes campaign films, brand documentaries and content series for clients across the UK and Europe. The room is bigger. The refusal is the same.

— Why us

/03

We show up, we listen, and we deliver. Every time.

One director, start to finish

The director who hears your brief is the person who calls cut on set and approves the final grade. No handoffs, no lost context.

Twenty-one days

Most brand films go from confirmed brief to final delivery in three weeks. Fast because we are organised, not because we are rushing.

The suite comes as standard

Every production includes a hero film and a full cutdown suite: 60, 30, 15 and 6 seconds. One shoot day, one budget, every channel.

Six projects at a time

We cap ourselves at six live projects. Every frame that leaves the studio has been seen, questioned and approved by a senior director.
