On the first Saturday of every month the studio shop opens at ten with a table of pots that did not make it onto the website. They are called seconds, they are 30% off, and they are some of our favourite things we make.

What counts as a second

  • A rim that dipped while the pot was drying, so it is a few millimetres out of true.
  • A glaze that ran and left a thick bead near the foot.
  • A speck of kiln grit that landed in the glaze and fired in.
  • A handle set a touch off centre. You will only notice if you look.

None of these affect how a pot works. It holds coffee, it goes in the dishwasher, it lasts. What they affect is our own standard for what goes out under the Hollowell name with a full price on it.

What does not count

Cracks. A crack is not a second, it is the bucket. We do not sell anything that will fail in use, at any price, because a pot that breaks in a hand or a dishwasher is a pot we will hear about.

Why studio only

Seconds are all different, and the difference is the point. You need to pick the one whose wonk you like. Photographing each one, writing a listing and posting it would cost more than the 30% we take off, and it would take the fun out of it. So it is a table, a kettle, and whoever turns up first.
