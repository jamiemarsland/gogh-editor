Since 2011

A short history.

2011

A borrowed camera, a summer on the coast and a habit of photographing the hour before dinner. The first prints sold from a café wall.

2016

The studio opened above a bakery. Portraits by day, editorial work by night, and the first wedding that felt like a long lunch.

2021

Mara joined, then Tom. The van was bought, the assistants of assistants were not. The work slowed down on purpose.

Now

People, places and the quiet in between, for anyone who would rather keep a moment than perform one.
